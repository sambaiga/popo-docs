popo.network#

Circuits as defined: elements, customers and feeder screening. See Network.

Circuits and their elements

Circuit

The circuit currently compiled in OpenDSS.

Line

A line or service cable as the circuit defines it.

Load

A load at its defined (not time-varying) rating.

Transformer

A distribution transformer as the circuit defines it.

PVSystem

A rooftop array: inverter rating, array rating and connection.

StorageUnit

A battery: power and energy rating, connection and current state.

Customers and transformers

residential_loads

Enumerate the low-voltage residential loads on a live circuit.

transformer_customers

The customer buses each transformer supplies, found by walking its low-voltage network.

read_transformers

Read transformers from an OpenDSS definition file.

select_by_utilisation

Choose the transformers to record, held fixed across scenarios.

screen

Peak apparent power and voltage extremes per transformer over a window.

Describing a feeder

profile_circuit

Describe a compiled circuit at its defined loading.

CircuitProfile

What a circuit contains, at its defined loading.

measure_sensitivity

Measure how far the worst bus voltage moves when the loading is raised.

VoltageSensitivity

How far the worst bus voltage moves per unit of substation demand.

attach_weather

Join weather columns onto a result frame, for use as forecasting inputs.

MVVoltageProfile

MV busbar voltage, anti-correlated with regional demand.
