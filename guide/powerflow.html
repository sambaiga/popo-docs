Power flow#

popo.powerflow solves a circuit over time and reads its results back as tables.

Results as tables#

Each function takes the opendssdirect module with a solved circuit and returns a long frame, one row per element and phase.

Function

One row per

bus_voltages()

bus and phase, labelled by the phase it is connected on

line_currents()

line and phase

line_losses()

line

element_powers()

element and phase, for loads, lines, transformers, PV or storage

circuit_summary()

circuit: convergence, counts and total losses

Circuit has the same tables as methods: circuit.bus_voltages() and so on.

Time series#

solve_time_series() steps the circuit through its load shapes, recording power and voltage at each monitored transformer’s secondary and at the feeder head. Passing buses= also records a voltage trace at every customer, in the same run.

report = solve_time_series(dss, transformers, horizon=96, buses=customer_buses)
report.require_convergence()              # raise if any step diverged
traces = report.voltage_traces()          # bus -> voltage per step

The returned SolveReport carries how many steps did not converge. A diverged step’s customer voltage is recorded as NaN, not as the solver’s last guess.

Sweeps#

run_uk_sweep() and run_us_sweep() sweep EV and PV penetration on a feeder, building a fresh circuit per scenario from the same seed, so the customers who hold PV at 40 % still hold it at 60 % and the sweep is a controlled comparison.
