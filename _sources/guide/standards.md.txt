# Standards

{py:mod}`popo.standards` holds voltage limits and inverter characteristics as named standards rather than
numbers, so a study records which standard it applied.

## Voltage limits

| Constructor | Band | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| {py:meth}`VoltageLimits.en_50160 <popo.standards.VoltageLimits.en_50160>` | 0.90 to 1.10 pu | 95 % of samples, no sample past +10 %/-15 % |
| {py:meth}`VoltageLimits.ansi_c84_1_range_a <popo.standards.VoltageLimits.ansi_c84_1_range_a>` | 0.95 to 1.05 pu | every sample |
| {py:meth}`VoltageLimits.ansi_c84_1_range_b <popo.standards.VoltageLimits.ansi_c84_1_range_b>` | 0.917 to 1.058 pu | every sample |

```python
limits = VoltageLimits.en_50160()
limits.check(voltages["vmag_pu"]).summary()     # the verdict, and why
limits.violations(voltages)                      # the readings outside the band, and by how much
limits.event_summary(report.voltage_traces())    # per customer: how often, how far, how long
```

`event_summary` separates a customer who briefly touches a limit from one who sits outside it for hours.

## Inverters

- {py:class}`~popo.standards.InverterCapability`: reactive capability, by standard (IEEE 1547 category B,
  EREC G99).
- {py:class}`~popo.standards.VoltVarCurve`: reactive power against terminal voltage.
- {py:class}`~popo.standards.VoltWattCurve`: active power curtailed against terminal voltage, with the IEEE
  1547 default.
