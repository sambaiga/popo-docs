.. module:: popo.plotting.tokens

{py:mod}`popo.plotting.tokens` holds the palette, `PALETTE`, and the colours named by role: `PRIMARY` for
the subject of a figure, `INFO` and `WARNING` for supporting series, `DANGER` for a failure and
`TEXT_MUTED` for context.
