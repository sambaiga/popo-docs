Tutorials
=========

Three end-to-end notebooks, each running on real feeders that ship with popo. They are executed every time
these pages are built, so every number and figure below comes from the current code.

**Modelling** (01)
   Build a low-voltage circuit in OpenDSS, read it back with ``Circuit`` and the result tables, check it
   against a statutory limit, and work through three network-expansion exercises.

**Time series and DER** (02)
   A real 188-customer UK feeder with real household, PV and EV profiles: a two-day time series, a
   hosting-capacity sweep, every customer's voltage alongside the substation's, and Volt-VAr, Volt-Watt and
   storage compared.

**State estimation** (03)
   The same feeder estimated from what is measured: the substation, smart meters and line sensors, and how
   many of each are enough.
