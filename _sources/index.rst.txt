   <!-- ── Why popo ──────────────────────────────────────────── -->
   <div class="po-why">
     <p class="po-why-lead">
       Low-voltage networks are barely measured: a meter at the substation and little
       downstream, while the decisions that matter, how much solar or how many electric
       vehicles a feeder can take, depend on every customer's connection. popo simulates
       what a network would do under a given level of uptake, and reconstructs what it
       is doing from what little is metered.
     </p>

     <div class="po-feature-grid">

       <div class="po-fcard">
         <div class="po-fcard-header">
           <span class="po-feature-dot" style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#107591,#0b5a70)">
             <i class="bi bi-diagram-3-fill"></i>
           </span>
           <h3 class="po-fcard-title">Networks as Python, not as COM calls</h3>
         </div>
         <p class="po-fcard-body"><code>Circuit</code> reads a compiled OpenDSS circuit back
         as records: lines, loads, transformers, PV and storage. Results come back as long
         tables, one row per element and phase, with every bus labelled by the phase it is
         actually on.</p>
         <a href="guide/network.html" class="po-fcard-link">Network &amp; Circuit <i class="bi bi-arrow-right"></i></a>
       </div>

       <div class="po-fcard">
         <div class="po-fcard-header">
           <span class="po-feature-dot" style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#00c0bf,#068f8e)">
             <i class="bi bi-lightning-charge-fill"></i>
           </span>
           <h3 class="po-fcard-title">Time series with convergence reported</h3>
         </div>
         <p class="po-fcard-body"><code>solve_time_series</code> steps a feeder through real
         profiles, records the substation and, optionally, every customer's voltage, and
         returns how many steps did not converge, rather than leaving it to a log line.</p>
         <a href="guide/powerflow.html" class="po-fcard-link">Power flow &amp; results <i class="bi bi-arrow-right"></i></a>
       </div>

       <div class="po-fcard">
         <div class="po-fcard-header">
           <span class="po-feature-dot" style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#f69a48,#d9741f)">
             <i class="bi bi-sun-fill"></i>
           </span>
           <h3 class="po-fcard-title">DER uptake and mitigation</h3>
         </div>
         <p class="po-fcard-body">Attach real household, PV and EV profiles at a given
         penetration, each on its customer's own phase, sweep hosting capacity, and compare
         Volt-VAr, Volt-Watt and battery storage on the same customers.</p>
         <a href="guide/scenarios.html" class="po-fcard-link">DER &amp; mitigation <i class="bi bi-arrow-right"></i></a>
       </div>

       <div class="po-fcard">
         <div class="po-fcard-header">
           <span class="po-feature-dot" style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#525252,#2b2b2b)">
             <i class="bi bi-check-circle-fill"></i>
           </span>
           <h3 class="po-fcard-title">Standards, not bare numbers</h3>
         </div>
         <p class="po-fcard-body">EN 50160, ANSI C84.1, IEEE 1547 and EREC G99 as named
         objects carrying their own rules. A verdict says why, <code>violations</code> says
         where, and <code>event_summary</code> separates a brief touch from an afternoon
         over the limit.</p>
         <a href="guide/standards.html" class="po-fcard-link">Standards <i class="bi bi-arrow-right"></i></a>
       </div>

       <div class="po-fcard">
         <div class="po-fcard-header">
           <span class="po-feature-dot" style="background:linear-gradient(135deg,#1b2329,#107591)">
             <i class="bi bi-speedometer2"></i>
           </span>
           <h3 class="po-fcard-title">State estimation for unmeasured feeders</h3>
         </div>
         <p class="po-fcard-body">Convert a feeder to pandapower and check it against
         OpenDSS, count whether a measurement set can determine the state, and estimate every
         bus from the substation, smart meters and line sensors.</p>
         <a href="guide/estimation.html" class="po-fcard-link">State estimation <i class="bi bi-arrow-right"></i></a>
       </div>

       <div class="po-fcard">
         <div class="po-fcard-header">

   <div class="po-companion">
     <p><strong>popo</strong> is Swahili for bat, and a companion to
     <a href="https://sambaiga.github.io/twiga-docs/">twiga</a>, Swahili for giraffe.
     twiga sees far, forecasting ahead in time; popo sees in the dark, inferring the state
     of a network that is not measured.</p>
   </div>
