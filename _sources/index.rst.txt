       <h2 class="po-task-title">Simulate</h2>
       <p class="po-task-body">Step a feeder through household, PV and EV profiles, recording transformer power,
       feeder-head power and every customer's voltage at each step.</p>
       <a class="po-task-link" href="guide/powerflow.html">Read the user guide <i class="bi bi-arrow-right"></i></a>
     </div>

       <h2 class="po-task-title">Estimate</h2>
       <p class="po-task-body">Convert a feeder to pandapower, check that the measurements can determine its state, and

       <a class="po-task-link" href="guide/estimation.html">Read the user guide <i class="bi bi-arrow-right"></i></a>
