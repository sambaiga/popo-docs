   <div class="po-tasks">

     <div class="po-task">
       <a class="po-task-figure" href="tutorials/notebooks/01-opendss-lv-modeling.html">
         <img src="_static/img/panel-model.svg"
              alt="Tutorial 01's six-house network at peak load, each bus coloured by voltage status." />

       <h2 class="po-task-title">Model</h2>
       <p class="po-task-body">Load an OpenDSS circuit as Python records and read its results
       back as tables, one row per element and phase, with each voltage checked against a
       named standard. On the six-house network of tutorial 01 at peak load, the farthest
       house is at 0.920 pu and the nearest at 0.957 pu.</p>
       <p class="po-task-tools"><span>Key tools:</span>
         <a href="api/index.html#popo.network.Circuit"><code>Circuit</code></a>,
         <a href="api/index.html#popo.powerflow.bus_voltages"><code>bus_voltages</code></a>,
         <a href="api/index.html#popo.standards.VoltageLimits"><code>VoltageLimits</code></a></p>
       <a class="po-task-link" href="guide/network.html">Read the user guide <i class="bi bi-arrow-right"></i></a>
     </div>

     <div class="po-task">
       <a class="po-task-figure" href="tutorials/notebooks/02-timeseries-der-modeling.html">
         <img src="_static/img/panel-simulate.svg"
              alt="Voltage over two days at 80% PV: the substation busbar stays near 1.04 pu while the most exposed customer rises above the 1.10 pu limit." />
       </a>
       <h2 class="po-task-title">Simulate</h2>
       <p class="po-task-body">Step a feeder through half-hourly household, PV and EV profiles,
       recording the voltage at every customer as well as at the substation. At 80% PV on a
       188-customer UK feeder, the busbar never exceeds 1.048 pu, while the most exposed
       customer reaches 1.156 pu and stays above the EN 50160 limit for four and a half hours.</p>
       <p class="po-task-tools"><span>Key tools:</span>
         <a href="api/index.html#popo.powerflow.solve_time_series"><code>solve_time_series</code></a>,
         <a href="api/index.html#popo.scenarios.attach_generation"><code>attach_generation</code></a>,
         <a href="api/index.html#popo.standards.VoltWattCurve"><code>VoltWattCurve</code></a></p>
       <a class="po-task-link" href="guide/powerflow.html">Read the user guide <i class="bi bi-arrow-right"></i></a>
     </div>

     <div class="po-task">
       <a class="po-task-figure" href="tutorials/notebooks/03-lv-state-estimation.html">
         <img src="_static/img/panel-estimate.svg"
              alt="True and estimated voltage at each of the feeder's buses, ordered by true voltage; the two agree throughout." />
       </a>
       <h2 class="po-task-title">Estimate</h2>
       <p class="po-task-body">Convert a feeder to pandapower, check that a set of measurements
       can determine its state, and estimate the voltage at every bus by weighted least squares.
       With every customer on the same feeder metered, the estimate is within 0.001 pu at every
       bus; with two customers unmetered, no estimate exists.</p>
       <p class="po-task-tools"><span>Key tools:</span>
         <a href="api/index.html#popo.estimation.to_pandapower"><code>to_pandapower</code></a>,
         <a href="api/index.html#popo.estimation.observability"><code>observability</code></a>,
         <a href="api/index.html#popo.estimation.estimate_voltages"><code>estimate_voltages</code></a></p>
       <a class="po-task-link" href="guide/estimation.html">Read the user guide <i class="bi bi-arrow-right"></i></a>
     </div>
